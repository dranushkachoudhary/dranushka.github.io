About the Author

Meet Dr. Anushka Choudhary

BDS Dentist · Dental Educator

I believe good dental care begins with understanding. Patients deserve to know what is happening, why a treatment is being recommended, and what they can expect from it.

Why I created this space

As a dentist, I have seen how many questions patients have about their teeth, gums and dental treatments. Sometimes the information is available, but it is buried under complicated terminology that can make oral health feel much more confusing than it needs to be.

I created this space to make dental education easier to understand - whether you're a patient looking for an answer to a common dental question or simply someone who wants to take better care of their oral health.

What you'll find here

You'll find practical, evidence-based information about common dental problems, oral hygiene, dental procedures, myths, prevention and the questions patients ask most often.

My goal is simple: to help you understand your oral health better, make informed decisions and feel more confident when talking to your dentist.

My approach

I try to approach dental education the same way I approach a conversation with a patient: without unnecessary jargon, without fear, and without making simple questions feel silly.

Not every dental concern has the same answer, and online information can never replace an individual examination. But I hope this website can help you ask better questions, understand your options and feel more comfortable discussing your oral health with your dentist.
