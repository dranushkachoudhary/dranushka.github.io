Meet Dr. Anushka

Hi, I'm
Dr. Anushka.

Dentist. Learner. And a firm believer that dental information shouldn't be intimidating.

I'm a BDS graduate with a simple goal — to make conversations about dental health easier to understand.

Why I started this

Somewhere between the dental chair and the internet, I noticed something.

Patients have questions.

A lot of them.

“Why are my gums bleeding?”

“Does scaling make teeth loose?”

“Why is my tooth sensitive?”

“Do I really need this treatment?”

And sometimes, patients don't ask these questions at all. They simply go home with a worry they don't know how to explain.

At the same time, the internet is full of dental advice — some helpful, some confusing, and some simply wrong.

That's why I wanted to create a space where dental information could feel different.

Simple. Honest. Evidence-informed. And written for an actual person, rather than a textbook.

My approach

I don't think patients should just know
what they're getting.

I think they deserve to understand why. Good dental care isn't only about treatment. It's also about communication, trust and helping people feel comfortable asking questions.

Simple

Dental concepts explained in everyday language, without unnecessary jargon.

Honest

No exaggerated promises, no unnecessary fear — just a balanced conversation about oral health.

Patient-first

Because every patient deserves to understand what is happening and why it matters.

Let's make dental health
a little less confusing.

Whether you're curious about a dental myth, worried about something you've noticed, or simply want to understand your oral health better — you're welcome here.
