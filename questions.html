Questions I've Answered

From the questions patients ask.

“Does dental scaling loosen your teeth?”

Professional dental scaling does not loosen healthy teeth. Sometimes teeth can feel different after deposits are removed, especially when there was already gum inflammation or supporting tissue loss. The important thing is to understand what is happening underneath the gums rather than avoiding necessary dental cleaning.

— Dr. Anushka Choudhary, BDS
“Why do my gums bleed when I brush?”

Bleeding while brushing is not something you should simply ignore. It can be associated with gum inflammation, plaque buildup or other oral health problems. If bleeding continues, becomes frequent or is accompanied by swelling or discomfort, a dental examination can help identify the cause.

— Dr. Anushka Choudhary, BDS
“Why are my teeth yellow even though I brush twice a day?”

Teeth are not naturally pure white. Their natural colour can vary, and factors such as enamel thickness, the underlying dentin, staining and plaque can affect how they look. Good brushing is important, but tooth colour alone does not tell you whether your teeth are healthy.

— Dr. Anushka Choudhary, BDS
