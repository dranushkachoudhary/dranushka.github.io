Dental Disclaimer

A few important things to know about the information shared on this website.

Educational information only

The information published on this website is provided for general educational and informational purposes only.

The articles are intended to help readers better understand common dental topics, oral health concepts, dental treatments, and questions that patients may have.

Information on this website is not a substitute for an examination, diagnosis, treatment plan, or personalised advice from a qualified dental or healthcare professional.

Every patient is different

Dental conditions can have different causes and may require different approaches depending on an individual's medical history, dental history, symptoms, examination findings, radiographs, and other clinical information.

Therefore, information that applies to one person may not necessarily apply to another.

Please do not use information from this website to diagnose yourself or another person, or to make decisions about starting, stopping, or changing treatment without consulting an appropriate healthcare professional.

When you should see a dentist

If you have a dental concern, persistent pain, swelling, bleeding, sensitivity, trauma, a change in the appearance of a tooth, or another symptom that worries you, please arrange an assessment with a qualified dentist.

In situations involving severe pain, significant swelling, facial trauma, difficulty breathing or swallowing, or other potentially serious symptoms, seek appropriate urgent medical or dental care.

No dentist-patient relationship

Reading information on this website, sending a message, submitting a question, or otherwise communicating through this website does not by itself establish a dentist-patient relationship between you and Dr. Anushka Choudhary.

Accuracy and updates

Every reasonable effort is made to provide information that is accurate, understandable, and based on reputable dental and healthcare sources at the time of publication.

However, dental and medical knowledge can change over time. Information may also become outdated, incomplete, or inappropriate for a particular individual.

No guarantee is made that every piece of information on this website will always be complete, current, or applicable to every situation.

External links and references

Some articles may include references or links to external websites, organisations, publications, or resources.

These resources are provided to help readers explore topics further. Their inclusion does not necessarily mean that every statement, service, product, or recommendation on an external website is endorsed by Dr. Anushka Choudhary.

Images and illustrations

Images and illustrations used on this website are intended to support general educational explanations. They may not represent the exact clinical appearance of every dental condition or treatment.

Professional responsibility

This website is created with the goal of making dental information easier to understand and helping patients feel more informed when discussing their oral health with their dentist.

It is not intended to replace professional clinical care. When in doubt about your dental health, please speak directly with a qualified dental professional.
