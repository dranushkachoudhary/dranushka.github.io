Don't Ignore These Signs

When should you see a dentist promptly?

Some dental problems should not be left to "see if they get better." Consider seeking prompt professional dental care if you experience:

  • Severe or persistent tooth pain
  • Facial or jaw swelling
  • Fever along with dental pain or swelling
  • Difficulty swallowing or breathing
  • A tooth that becomes loose unexpectedly
  • Bleeding that does not stop
  • A mouth ulcer or lesion that does not heal
