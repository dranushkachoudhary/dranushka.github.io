Dental Treatments

Dental treatment,
explained simply.

Dental treatments can sound complicated or intimidating. This section explains common procedures in simple language — what they are, why they may be recommended and what they are generally intended to do.

Explore Treatments

Start with the treatment you're curious about.

Every dental treatment has a purpose. Understanding what a procedure involves can make dental visits feel less confusing and help you have better conversations with your dentist.

Dental Fillings

Fillings are commonly used to restore teeth affected by cavities or certain forms of tooth damage. They help restore the shape and function of the tooth.

Root Canal Treatment

Root canal treatment is used when the soft tissue inside a tooth becomes irreversibly inflamed or infected. The goal is to clean and seal the inside of the tooth while preserving it.

Important to remember

Not every dental problem needs the same treatment.

The right treatment depends on the condition of the tooth or gums, the extent of the problem, your overall oral health and several other factors. A dental examination is often needed before deciding which treatment is appropriate.
