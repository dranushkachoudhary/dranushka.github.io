Important

Not every tooth needs the same treatment.

The right treatment depends on the condition of the tooth, your symptoms, clinical examination and sometimes dental X-rays. Treatment should always be personalised rather than chosen based on symptoms alone.

Before Treatment

How does a dentist decide what you need?

A dental treatment recommendation is usually based on more than just whether something hurts. Your dentist considers several factors before deciding what would be appropriate.

01

Your Symptoms

What you are experiencing — such as pain, sensitivity, swelling or bleeding — provides important information.

02

Clinical Examination

Your dentist examines the teeth, gums, bite and surrounding tissues to understand what may be happening.

03

Dental X-rays

When needed, X-rays can reveal problems that cannot be seen during a routine examination.
